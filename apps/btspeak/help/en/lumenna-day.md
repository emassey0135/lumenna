# Lumenna Day

The day as it is lived, opening on now: blocks in time order with the tasks assigned to them underneath, the free time between them, and a line for now. The title says the day and sums it up.

## Enter

- On a block: edit it. A repeating block asks whether to change that day only or every occurrence.
- On a task in a block: edit the task, as Edit details does.
- On free time: add a block there.
- On a day cancelled from a repeating block: put it back.

## A row's context menu, M-Chord with Dot 7

On a block:

- Assign a task, i, to a block that takes tasks.
- Edit block, e: its name, times and kind, whether it takes tasks, counts toward hours for work or is anchored, and for every occurrence its repetition, notes, shortest length, the filter its tasks come from, its last day and its colour. Changing the kind brings that kind's own settings, unless you changed them too.
- Cancel this day, x, for a repeating block. Restore this day, o, for a day changed on its own. Delete block, also Control-D.
- On the block under way, End now, and Add 15 minutes.
- Carry the rest forward, f, on a block that left more than one task short: each goes to the next block that takes it.

On a task in a block, one sitting of it:

- Start timer, Pause timer, or Resume timer, s. Pausing keeps the time so far.
- Stop timer, t, ending the sitting: whether it is running or paused.
- Planned length, l: how long this sitting is meant to take, such as 45m. Leave it empty for none.
- Log minutes, m: the whole of the sitting, replacing what was logged.
- Edit task details, e. Unassign, also Control-D.
- Carry forward, f, on a sitting left short with its task still open: choose the block it goes to.

On free time, Add block here, a. On a cancelled day, Restore this day, o.

## The day's main menu, M-Chord

- First, what the day offers itself, as it does: Plan the day; Replan from now; What should I work on?, w, which offers the tasks to start on, each saying after a moment why it is suggested; End the day; Clear the rest of the day.
- Add a block, a. Previous day, p. Next day, n. Go to a day, g: any date you can say, such as "next friday". Today, t.
- Undo, u, and Redo, y.

## Replan from now and Plan the day

The title says the whole proposal before anything changes. Each change is a row, checked to begin with; Enter turns it off or on, and a sitting of Plan the day says after a moment why its task was chosen. Make the changes checked, or Plan the ones checked, makes them, and also says after a moment what becomes of one left unchecked: the core proposes again around it, and the new proposal is shown, until nothing more is proposed. Cancel leaves, making nothing. Both are on the main menu too. Under Other options, Replan from now lists up to three other ways through the day, each saying what it costs; Enter on one shows its proposal in place.

## Blocks

The Blocks list on the main menu holds every block. Enter edits every occurrence of one; Control-D deletes it, asking first; a adds one.
