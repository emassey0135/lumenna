# Lumenna Tasks

A list of tasks, as a tree: subtasks sit under their task, and the level is said where it changes. The title says how the list's filter was understood, then how many tasks it holds.

Press Enter on a task to read its details. Press Z-Chord to leave the list.

## A task's context menu, M-Chord with Dot 7

- Complete, c; or Mark not done, for a finished one.
- Edit, e: the title, due date, repetition, priority, estimate, project, labels and notes, one form.
- Details: everything about the task, to pan through.
- Put it in a block, b: a work block of the coming week, or Another day for any other, and how long the sitting is meant to take.
- Move to a project, m.
- Make it a subtask of another task, s; Move it to the top level, t.
- Wait for another task, w; Stop waiting for another task, n.
- Delete: to the Trash. Control-D and the D chord do the same.

## The list's main menu, M-Chord

- Add a task, a. In a project's or label's list, the line starts with it, so the task lands there.
- Search or filter, slash: a filter such as "#Work & overdue", or "search:" and words to look through titles and notes.
- Undo, u, and Redo, y.

## Writing a task

Write it the way you would say it: "water the plants every monday", "draft the report friday at 2pm p2 #Work @writing 45m". What was understood is said back before it is added. A project that does not exist stops the task; a label that does not exist becomes a new label.

## The Trash

Enter restores a task. Its context menu also has Erase for good, which asks first and cannot be undone; Control-D and the D chord do the same.
