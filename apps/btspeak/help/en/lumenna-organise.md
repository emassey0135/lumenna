# Lumenna Projects, Labels and Filters

Projects, labels and saved filters are on Home, each under its heading: Projects, p; Labels, l; Saved filters, f. Enter on a heading folds or unfolds what is under it; its context menu, M-Chord with Dot 7, adds one: New project, New label, New saved filter.

## Projects

The project tree, with each project's open tasks, its weight and whether it is archived. Enter shows a project's tasks. Its context menu:

- Add a task to it.
- Rename, r. New project inside. Move under, m. Move to top level, for one inside another.
- Move up, comma, and Move down, period, where there is somewhere to go.
- Weight: how much the whole project matters now, roughly 0.5 to 2, or "inherit".
- Archive or Unarchive. Delete, which asks whether its tasks go to the Trash or to the Inbox.

On Home, n is Sync now and w is What should I work on?, so New project inside and Weight are reached from the context menu. Inside a project's tasks, its menu, M-Chord, has New project inside, p.

The Inbox keeps its name and its place, so it offers only moving and Weight.

## Labels

Enter shows the tasks wearing a label. Its context menu: Add a task wearing it. Rename, r. Merge into, m, for a near-duplicate made by a typo. Colour, c: a colour name, or none; the name always shows too. Move up and Move down. Delete, which leaves the tasks as they are.

## Saved filters

Enter opens a filter's tasks. A filter is kept as you typed it, so "today" means today whenever it is opened. Its context menu: Rename, r. Change query, q. Move up and Move down. Delete. To filter without saving, use Filter tasks, slash, in a list of tasks.
