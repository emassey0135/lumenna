# Lumenna Projects, Labels and Filters

## Projects

The project tree, with each project's open tasks, its weight and whether it is archived. Enter shows a project's tasks. Its context menu, M-Chord with Dot 7:

- Show its tasks. Add a task to it, t.
- Rename, r. New project inside, n. Move under, m. Move to top level, for one inside another.
- Move up, comma, and Move down, period, where there is somewhere to go.
- Weight, w: how much the whole project matters now, roughly 0.5 to 2, or "inherit".
- Archive or Unarchive. Delete, which asks whether its tasks go to the Trash or to the Inbox.

The Inbox keeps its name and its place, so it offers only moving and Weight. The main menu, M-Chord, has New project, a. Inside a project's tasks, its main menu also has New project inside, p.

## Labels

Enter shows the tasks wearing a label. Its context menu: Show the tasks wearing it. Add a task wearing it, t. Rename, r. Merge into, m, for a near-duplicate made by a typo. Colour, c: a colour name, or none; the name always shows too. Move up and Move down. Delete, which leaves the tasks as they are. The main menu has New label, a.

## Saved filters

Enter opens a filter's tasks. A filter is kept as you typed it, so "today" means today whenever it is opened. Its context menu: Rename, r. Change query, q. Move up and Move down. Delete. The main menu has New saved filter, a, and Search or filter now, slash.
