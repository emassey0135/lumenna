# Lumenna Reminders

## A task's or a block's reminders

A task with a due date and every block offer Reminders, r, in their context menu. The list says how many reminders there are and whether they are the task's own or the defaults from Settings. The main menu has Add reminder, a: type when, such as 5m before start, at end, or 30m before. If Lumenna cannot read it, it says why and asks again with what you typed. Control-D removes one of its own; a default is changed in Settings, Planning.

## What came due

Reminders, r, on the main menu, lists what came due over the last day and was not dismissed. Control-D dismisses one, on every device. Its context menu also has Snooze 10 minutes, z, and, where a block ended with work left short and the reminder asks whether to carry it on, Carry forward, f; and where a block's work outruns its time, Replan, p, which opens Replan from now.

## Reminders on this device

In Settings, Reminders, on this device: Reminders on this device turns them off here alone; every other device still gives them. The reminder service gives reminders while Lumenna is closed, with the notification sound, speech and braille. Enter on it installs it, asking first, or removes it. It needs an administrator.
